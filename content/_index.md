I am a reasearch assistant of Computer Science at the Technical University of Darmstadt working towards my PhD under Prof. Reiner Hähnle in the Software Engineering Group. My main focus of study is formal verification of Rust programs using Dynamic Logic and the KeY system. I also work on formal, trace-based semantics following the _locally abstract, globally concrete_ (LAGC) approach.

Additionally, I also work (as a part-time contractor) for the [Typst](https://typst.app) team, in particular on its bibliography tool, [Hayagriva](https://github.com/typst/hayagriva).

On here I write about whatever interests me (once I find the time). This includes politics, philosophy, web development, computer science, and open source software.

You can also follow me on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/DerDrodtel) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/daniel-drodt-95a960159/), if you want. I rarely post, however.
